Cabana takeovers for the people you love.

Six curtained cabanas, each seating up to eight, can be booked privately for birthdays, anniversaries or proposals. The chef builds a tasting menu around your favourite flavours, our sommelier pairs it, and the team takes care of everything from cake to flowers.
