Events Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I host a private birthday party at Farzi Café Dehradun?
Yes. Farzi Café Dehradun hosts birthdays regularly, with options including private cabana bookings for up to 8 guests, half-rooftop or full-rooftop buyouts. The team arranges cake reveals, décor, projection signage and custom cocktails.
How do I book a private event or corporate dinner at Farzi Café Dehradun?
WhatsApp or call +91 76177 71124, or use the enquiry form on this page. The events team replies within an hour during business hours. Include your occasion, date, approximate guest count and any special requirements.
How many guests do the private cabanas at Farzi Café Dehradun accommodate?
There are six private curtained cabanas, each seating up to 8 guests comfortably. Two cabanas can be combined for a private wing. The full rooftop can accommodate 120 seated or 200 standing for larger buyouts.
What occasions does Farzi Café Dehradun host?
The events team regularly hosts birthday celebrations, anniversary dinners, corporate off-sites and client dinners, kitty parties, engagements, sangeets, baby showers and full rooftop buyouts.
What is the minimum spend for a private cabana at Farzi Café Dehradun?
The minimum spend for a single private cabana starts from ₹ 18,000. This includes a complimentary dessert with sparkler and a name card. For group sizes, capacity and availability, contact the events team on +91 76177 71124.
Does Farzi Café Dehradun offer AV equipment for corporate events?
Yes. The AV-equipped private wing accommodates up to 60 guests for corporate events. The team also supports product launches and client dinners with late-night flexibility and invoicing.
