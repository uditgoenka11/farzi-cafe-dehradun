Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Farzi Café Dehradun and where can I park?
Farzi Café Dehradun is at 222, Dhakpatti, Rajpur Road, Dehradun — in the Dhakpatti area. Free valet parking is available at the restaurant.
What are Farzi Café Dehradun's opening hours?
Open daily from 12 PM to 11:30 PM. Same hours all seven days of the week.
How can I make a reservation at Farzi Café Dehradun?
WhatsApp or call +91 76177 71124. Reservations can also be submitted via the form on this page. The team replies on WhatsApp within the hour during business hours.
What is the best time to visit Farzi Café Dehradun for sunset rooftop seating?
Arrive between 6:00 PM and 6:30 PM in summer (sun drops behind Mussoorie around 6:50 PM), or 5:30 PM in winter. Specify 'open terrace' or 'cabana' when booking.
Is there valet parking at Farzi Café Dehradun?
Yes, free valet parking is available at the restaurant. The venue is also lift-accessible and wheelchair accessible.
How far in advance should I book a table at Farzi Café Dehradun?
For Friday and Saturday sunset slots, book by Wednesday. For weekday evenings, same-day booking usually works. WhatsApp +91 76177 71124 for the fastest response.
