Eat Your Way Through Dehradun in 24 Hours

By The Farzi Café Dehradun Team · 7 June 2026

If you're in Dehradun for one day and you want to eat the city properly, this is the itinerary the team here has refined over hundreds of off-days. It assumes you can walk between meals on Rajpur Road. It assumes you're willing to use the morning. It assumes you'll end up at Farzi for dinner, because we wrote it.

For a more detailed ranking of the city's best restaurants by category, see our full restaurant guide.

7:30 AM · Bun-tikki at Paltan Bazaar

The Doon breakfast is bun-tikki — a soft sweet bread bun split, slathered with butter and tomato-chilli chutney, stuffed with a hot aloo tikki. Five rupees of joy. Best carts: the western end of Paltan Bazaar, opposite the old clock tower. Get there before 9 AM. After that, the LBSNAA cadets descend.

Eat standing. Don't ask for cutlery. Wash it down with chai from the next stall.

10 AM · Coffee at Brewer's on EC Road

Walk it off. Brewer's roasts their own beans and the V60 setup is real. Order whatever they're recommending that week, eat an orange-polenta cake, look at the playlist (it's better than yours). Cash only.

12:30 PM · Garhwali thali at Kumar's, Rajpur

Two kilometres from us at 222 Dhakpatti, on the older stretch of Rajpur Road, sits Kumar's — an unmarked Garhwali thali joint that does mandua roti, gahat dal, kafuli and bhang ki chutney. Lunch service starts at 12:30 and ends at 3 PM. No takeaway. No menu. Sit, eat, walk it off.

This is the most Dehradun lunch you'll have. Don't skip it.

3 PM · Nap. Or Mall Road, Mussoorie.

This is when locals nap. If you don't, take the 45-minute drive up Rajpur Road to Mussoorie — Char Dukan momos at the top of Landour, walk down Mall Road, drive back by 5:30 PM.

6 PM · Sundowner cocktails at Farzi

This is where we'd want you. The rooftop bar at 222, Dhakpatti, Rajpur Road. Order a Saffron Sour. Sit on the open terrace. Watch the sun drop behind Mussoorie. This is the best 90 minutes of the day, every day, in May and June.

8 PM · Dinner at Farzi (yes, still here)

Don't move. The kitchen serves till 11 PM. Start with the Galouti on a Stone. Move to the Dal Chawal Arancini. Order the Awadhi Lamb Dum Biryani for the table. Finish with the Mishti Doi Tres Leches. Order one more cocktail. Stay till the cabanas light up at 10.

11 PM · Sahranpur Chowk for chow mein

If you're still hungry — and after a long evening you might be — the food carts at Sahranpur Chowk serve a thin Doon-style chow mein with extra vinegar and extra soy. It's ₹ 80. It comes in a paper bowl. It's the right way to end the night.

2 AM · Home, or your hotel, or the back seat of someone's car

That's the day.

The slightly fancier 24 hours

If you're not in the mood for bun-tikki and chow mein, here's the elevated version:

  • 10 AM — Brunch at Town Table or our Sunday Brunch at Farzi.
  • 1 PM — Lunch at Yeti — The Himalayan Kitchen (Tibetan-Bhutanese).
  • 5 PM — Coffee and a cake stop at Ellora's, Astley Hall.
  • 7 PM — Dinner at Farzi. Tasting menu. Two-hour commitment.
  • 10:30 PM — Stay for dessert and one last cocktail at the bar.

The cheap-but-honest 24 hours

Total cost: under ₹ 1,500.

  • 8 AM — Bun-tikki at Paltan Bazaar.
  • 12 PM — Kumar's thali (₹ 250).
  • 4 PM — Filter coffee and pastry at Ellora's.
  • 8 PM — Yeti's thukpa and momos.
  • 11 PM — Sahranpur Chowk chow mein.

Three menus, one city, one perfect day either way.
