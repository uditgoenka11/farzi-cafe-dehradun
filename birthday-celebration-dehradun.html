Birthday Celebration in Dehradun

From a cabana for eight to a full-rooftop blowout — how to plan the birthday Dehradun talks about for weeks.

Last updated: 12 June 2026

We run two to three birthdays a night on weekends. They're our most-booked event category, and they're also the most personal — every birthday has a story, a relationship, a reason. This guide walks through how to plan one at Farzi Café Dehradun (or, frankly, anywhere on Rajpur Road), what to expect, and what makes the difference between a "nice dinner" and a "best birthday ever."

This is specifically for birthdays from 10 to 200 guests, adult or teen, intimate to blowout. For kids' birthdays under 10, we'd point you toward farmhouse or play-zone venues — Farzi is a bar-led bistro and not the right room for kids' party games.

The five types of Dehradun birthdays — and where to host them

1. The intimate birthday — 6 to 10 guests

Where: A single curtained cabana at Farzi Café Dehradun. ₹ 18,000 minimum spend, includes cake reveal, candlelit setup, custom menu. Add personalised cocktails for ₹ 3,500. Best for: spouses, partners, close-circle friend dinners.

2. The friend-group birthday — 15 to 30 guests

Where: Two adjacent cabanas as a private wing, OR a long table on the open terrace. From ₹ 35,000. Includes shared platters, custom cocktail menu and projection signage with the birthday person's name. Best for: 25th, 30th, milestone birthdays among college/work friends.

3. The kitty / family birthday — 30 to 50 guests

Where: Half-rooftop reservation. From ₹ 1,20,000. Theme decor, DJ optional, photo wall, cake station. Best for: parents' milestone birthdays, large family birthdays, kitty group birthdays.

4. The milestone birthday — 50 to 120 guests

Where: Half or full rooftop buyout, DJ, dance floor, AV-equipped speech zone. From ₹ 2,00,000. Best for: 40th, 50th, 60th, 75th. These are big nights and they deserve the room.

5. The blowout / surprise — 100 to 200 guests

Where: Full rooftop buyout. From ₹ 4,00,000. Custom catering, live entertainment, projection video walls, dedicated event manager. Best for: husband-wife surprises, big-money corporate birthdays, sangeet-style birthdays.

What we do, by default, for every birthday booking

When you book a birthday at Farzi Café Dehradun — even just a basic cabana reservation — we automatically include:

  • A complimentary dessert with a sparkler. Brought out at a moment of your choosing. No cake-cutting charge.
  • The birthday song over the bar speakers. Yes, even the modern Indian version.
  • A custom signage card with the birthday person's name on the table.
  • A photographer call-out — the floor manager will tip our staff photographer to grab a few candid shots on his phone, sent to you the next morning.

How to plan a surprise birthday at Farzi Café Dehradun

Surprise birthdays are our specialty. The thing that distinguishes a good surprise from a great one is timing. Here's how it usually works:

  1. The "decoy" reservation. The host (e.g. partner, friend) books a normal dinner reservation. The celebrant thinks it's an ordinary night out.
  2. Pre-event walkthrough. The actual host visits us 1-2 days earlier, walks the space, finalises decor, hands over cake and any personal items.
  3. Guest staging. Surprise guests arrive 30 minutes earlier and are seated in a separate cabana or bistro corner. They stay quiet.
  4. The reveal moment. The host and celebrant arrive. The maître d' takes them on a small "tour" — to "show them a new section." That section is the surprise cabana. Lights up, music up, surprise.
  5. The evening continues. Cake, cocktails, projection slideshow, dinner, dancing if booked.

For surprise birthdays, give us at least 7 days notice. For big-cake or big-projection surprises, give us 14.

Birthday booking timeline

  1. 4 weeks before: WhatsApp first message. Confirm date, group size, budget bracket. Get a site visit invitation.
  2. 3 weeks before: Site visit. Choose cabana / half rooftop / full rooftop. Pick a menu direction. Discuss surprise logistics if relevant.
  3. 2 weeks before: Confirm menu, cake order, decor theme. Pay 30% advance.
  4. 1 week before: Final guest count, projection slides, music playlist, surprise timeline.
  5. The night: Show up. We handle everything else.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I celebrate a birthday in Dehradun?
Farzi Café Dehradun on Rajpur Road is the city's most-booked birthday venue. Private cabanas, custom cake arrangements, projection signage, dedicated event coordinators.
Do you take outside birthday cakes?
Yes — outside cakes are welcome. No cakeage charge for cakes under 3 kg.
Can you arrange surprise birthdays?
Yes. We run surprise reveals weekly. Our team handles timing, projection, cake reveal and decor while keeping the celebrant out of view till the moment. Give us 7-14 days notice.
What is the minimum spend for a birthday cabana?
A single private cabana has a ₹ 18,000 minimum spend, which includes complimentary dessert with sparkler, name card and a slot for cake-cutting.
Can I do a custom cocktail for the birthday?
Yes — our bar team will design 3-5 custom cocktails named after the birthday person. ₹ 3,500 design fee + drinks billed per pour.
Is Farzi Café Dehradun good for kids' birthdays?
For teens and adults, yes. For kids under 10, we'd point you toward farmhouse or play-zone venues — we're a bar-led bistro, not a kids' party venue.
