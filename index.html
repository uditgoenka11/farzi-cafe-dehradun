I · The Welcome

India, plated for the curious palate.

The name "Farzi" means illusion — and that's what we serve. Familiar Indian classics, reborn through technique, smoke and sleight-of-hand. A rooftop above Rajpur Road, with the Mussoorie hills as our quiet backdrop.

II · The Setting

A rooftop perched
above the foothills.

Brass canopies, an onyx-lit bar, curtained cabanas. Inside, hand-painted tiles and a glowing Farzi monogram. Outside, the line of hills runs all the way to Mussoorie.

Lunches by the glass wall. Sundowners at the bar. Dinners under string lights. Whichever Farzi you find — you'll want to come back.

View Gallery
III · The Kitchen

Familiar enough to recognise.
Redesigned enough to surprise.

Galouti on warm slate. Dal-chawal arancini. Awadhi biryani sealed under dough, broken open at the table. Mishti doi tres leches under a warm chocolate pour.

Modern Indian as we mean it — not fusion for its own sake, but classics tilted with care.

IV · The Bar

India's spice rack,
in a glass.

Saffron sours, curry-leaf gimlets, smoked-jaggery old-fashioneds, a Mussoorie hill highball that smells like a forest. India's most awarded cocktail programme, now in the foothills.
