Welcome to Farzi

Where modern India is plated, poured and lit.

✦

The name "Farzi" means illusion — and that's exactly what we serve. Familiar Indian classics reborn through molecular technique, smoke and sleight-of-hand. Now lighting up Rajpur Road from a rooftop with uninterrupted views of the Mussoorie hills.

The Farzi Experience

A Dining Experience Above The City

Six reasons Farzi Café has become Dehradun's most talked-about rooftop address.

Mountain Views

Unobstructed Mussoorie skyline framed by floor-to-ceiling glass and an open-air terrace.

Rooftop Dining

Brass-canopied tables, curtained cabanas and string-lit evenings high above Rajpur Road.

Signature Cocktails

India's spice rack in a glass — saffron sours, curry-leaf gimlets, smoked jaggery old-fashioneds.

Modern Indian Cuisine

Galouti on slate. Dal chawal arancini. Mishti doi tres leches. Classics tilted with technique.

Live Music

Acoustic sets on Fridays and DJ Saturdays. Always loud enough to enjoy, never loud enough to shout over.

Events

Birthdays, anniversaries, kitty parties, corporate evenings and full rooftop buyouts.

From the Pass

Signature plates

Six dishes that tell the Farzi story — each one a tilt of the head, a wink, a small theatre on a plate.

Galouti on a Stone

Slow-cooked melt-in-the-mouth lamb galouti, served on warm Himalayan slate with pickled onion and saffron emulsion.

Dal Chawal Arancini

The most beloved Indian comfort meal, rolled into crisp Italian arancini and finished with smoked tomato chutney.

Mishti Doi Tres Leches

Bengali sweet yogurt meets Latin tres leches under a hot chocolate pour at the table. Edible flowers, no compromise.

Deconstructed Ras Malai

Cardamom-poached chenna with pistachio crumble and a shard of burnt sugar — assembled live at your table.

Saffron Sour

House gin, fresh blood orange, Kashmiri saffron and citrus foam. Our most-photographed cocktail.

The Cabana Tasting

A 7-course chef's surprise paired with cocktails and wine, served in your own curtained cabana on the rooftop.

The Setting

A rooftop perched above the Himalayas.

Designer brass canopies, onyx-lit bars and curtained cabanas open onto an uninterrupted view of the Mussoorie hills. Inside, hand-painted tiles, neon mountains and a glowing Farzi monogram set the stage for evenings that drift.

Lunches by the glass wall. Sundowners at the bar. Dinners under string lights. Whichever Farzi you find — you'll want to come back.
