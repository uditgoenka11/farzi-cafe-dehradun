I · The Welcome

India, plated for the curious palate.

The name "Farzi" means illusion — and that's exactly what we serve. Familiar Indian classics, reborn through technique, smoke and sleight-of-hand. A rooftop above Rajpur Road, with the Mussoorie hills as our quiet backdrop. Brass canopies, an onyx-lit bar, curtained cabanas, and a kitchen that knows how to wink at a recipe without losing its soul.

II · The Setting

A rooftop perched
above the foothills.

Designer brass canopies, hand-painted tiles and a glowing Farzi monogram. Outside, the line of hills runs all the way to Mussoorie. Inside, evenings drift slowly — lunches by the glass wall, sundowners at the bar, dinners under string lights.

III · The Evening

Three rooms.
One quiet rooftop.

Whichever Farzi you find — bistro, bar, or cabana — you'll want to come back.

The Kitchen

Familiar enough to recognise.
Redesigned enough to surprise.

Galouti on warm slate. Dal-chawal arancini. Awadhi biryani sealed under dough, broken open at the table.

The Bar

India's spice rack,
in a glass.

Saffron sours. Curry-leaf gimlets. Smoked-jaggery old-fashioneds. A Mussoorie hill highball that smells like a forest.

The Cabanas

Curtained, candlelit,
made for the occasion.

Six private cabanas under brass canopies — for anniversaries, birthdays, surprise dinners, and slow conversations.

Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

Where is Farzi Café in Dehradun?
Farzi Café Dehradun is at 222, Dhakpatti, Rajpur Road, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248009 — in the Dhakpatti area. Free valet parking is available at the restaurant.
What kind of restaurant is Farzi Café Dehradun?
Farzi Café is a modern Indian bistro and rooftop bar — a concept by Massive Restaurants (founded by Zorawar Kalra). The kitchen serves contemporary interpretations of Indian classics alongside pizza, Asian plates, signature cocktails and crafted beverages.
What are Farzi Café Dehradun's opening hours?
Open daily from 1:30 PM to 11:55 PM. Same hours all seven days of the week.
How do I reserve a table at Farzi Café Dehradun?
WhatsApp or call +91 76177 71124. Weekend sunset slots typically fill up by Wednesday — early booking is recommended. You can also use the reservation form at contact.html.
Is there rooftop seating at Farzi Café Dehradun?
Yes — the restaurant features an open-air terrace with panoramic Mussoorie hill views, six private curtained cabanas, and a covered indoor bistro with floor-to-ceiling glass walls. All three zones offer the rooftop view.
What is the approximate cost for two at Farzi Café Dehradun?
Approximately ₹ 2,000 for two without alcohol, and around ₹ 3,500–4,000 for two with cocktails. The restaurant is in the premium price range.
